Write an email offering the expert a proposal draft prepared specifically for
their lab in connection with the funding round.

Use this exact structure, deriving every detail from the supplied funding round,
expert profile, and accepted proposal draft:
- Greet the expert by their first name when it is known.
- In one short paragraph, state the funding topic, available amount, and that a
  proposal draft was prepared for their lab.
- In one short paragraph, explain the concrete data, methods, or prior lab asset
  the draft builds on.
- Include two or three short hyphen-prefixed lines summarizing the proposal's
  concrete aims. These hyphen lines are the only exception to the general rule
  against bullets.
- State the proposal's requested budget and brief budget rationale when those
  facts are available. Never substitute the total funding-round amount for the
  proposal's requested budget.
- Include the exact draft review and application link from the supplied context.
- End with a low-pressure invitation to apply with the draft if the lab has
  bandwidth, and offer to answer questions.

Do not describe the draft as final. Do not praise the recipient. Do not mention
the AI or the process used to write the draft. Do not add facts that are absent
from the supplied context. Keep the email between 120 and 190 words, excluding
the signature.
